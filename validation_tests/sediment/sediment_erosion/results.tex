
\section{Entrainment of bed sediment into still water}

A tank of still water 1~m deep stands over a plane bed of slope
$S = 10^{-3}$. The sediment is 0.5~mm sand with the settling velocity $v_s$
of Ferguson and Church~\cite{FergusonChurch2004}. There is no flow, so the
case uses the depth--slope closure, under which the bed shear stress of a
cell depends only on its depth and bed slope, $\tau_b = \rho g h S$, with the
slope frozen at the setup bed, so that $\tau_b$ is a function of the depth
alone. The Shields stress is $\tau^{*} = hS/(RD) \approx 1.2$ with
$R = \rho_s/\rho - 1$, some $30\,\tau_c^{*}$ for the sand
($\tau_c^{*} = 0.04$), and the non-cohesive entrainment law of Smith and
McLean fires,
\begin{equation}
E = v_s E^{*}, \qquad
E^{*} = \frac{0.65\,\gamma_0 X}{1 + \gamma_0 X}, \qquad
X = \frac{\tau^{*}}{\tau_c^{*}} - 1, \qquad \gamma_0 = 0.0024 ,
\end{equation}
while deposition returns sediment to the bed at the well-mixed rate
$D = d^{*} v_s c$ with $d^{*} = 1$. A second fraction of the same grain but
$\tau_c^{*} = 10$ lies below its threshold and must stay at zero: the
control. Every cell is a closed system, so the sediment mass per unit area
$m = hc$ and the bed obey
\begin{equation}
\frac{dm}{dt} = E(h) - D, \qquad \frac{dz}{dt} = -\frac{E(h) - D}{1-\lambda},
\qquad h = w - z ,
\end{equation}
$\lambda = 0.3$: as the bed lowers the depth grows and $E$ with it. That
coupled system is integrated to machine precision for the reference, each
cell on its own curve; with the depth held fixed it reduces to a relaxation
$c(t) = c_{eq}(1 - e^{-t/T})$, $T = h/(d^{*}v_s) \approx 10$~s, which the
reference departs from as the bed moves. The slope is frozen on purpose:
read from the evolving bed, the depth--slope closure feeds on the roughness
that uneven erosion creates, and no closed-form reference exists; frozen,
$S$ is the reach slope the closure was written for and the bed can evolve
under it. The case checks the entrainment law and its threshold, the
depth--slope closure in every cell (the slope estimate must reproduce the
plane along the walls and in the corners as well as in the interior), the
balance of entrainment against deposition in the conserved sediment mass,
the bed lowering, and that sediment mass is conserved between the water
column and the bed.
